2. What the game stores on your device

Your progress, settings and saved runs are stored only on your device (and in your phone's own Google backup, if you have Android backup switched on). The game has no user accounts, and we do not receive this data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads, to deliver, measure and (with your consent) personalise ads.

When you first start the game in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, a consent message asks for your choice. You can change it any time under Settings → Privacy choices. Without consent, Google shows non-personalised ads only.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for personalised ads; our legitimate interest in financing the free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for non-personalised ads. More: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Analytics and error reports (GameAnalytics)

We use GameAnalytics (GameAnalytics ApS, Copenhagen, Denmark) to understand how the game is played and to find bugs. It collects a random device identifier, device and app information, and game events such as runs started and finished, matches played, ads watched and error messages. Like any internet service, its servers also receive your IP address. It does not collect your name, email or contacts.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks you first and sends nothing to GameAnalytics unless you agree. Everywhere, you can switch it off or on at any time under Settings → Play statistics.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). More: GameAnalytics privacy policy.

5. Purchases (Google Play)

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation that the unlock was bought, so the game can unlock it and restore it after a reinstall.

Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). More: Google Privacy Policy.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Google may process data in the USA. Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and also uses EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the game or its data. GameAnalytics and Google keep data according to their own retention periods (see their policies above).

8. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Settings → Privacy choices for ads, Settings → Play statistics for analytics). Because we cannot link analytics data to you by name, please include your device's GameAnalytics ID or a description of your device when you contact us.

You may also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen (LDI NRW).
